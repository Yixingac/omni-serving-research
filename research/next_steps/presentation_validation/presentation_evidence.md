# Presentation evidence index

> Every technical claim that may appear on a slide must have a row here. Columns: the claim as it may be said, the experiment, the measured result (with the run count), the figure, the evidence source in this repository, and the limitation that must be said with it.
> Evidence levels: **Measured** (read from a run), **Derived** (computed from measured values), **Estimated**, **Hypothesis**, **Unknown**, **Blocked**. A microbenchmark result is never an end-to-end result; a synthetic workload is never a real robot.
> Common conditions unless a row says otherwise: MiniCPM-o 4.5 (revision `fb09628e`), SGLang-Omni `520b49ee`, SGLang 0.5.21, torch 2.13.0, one node with 8× RTX A6000 48 GB (NVLink pairs 0-3, 1-2, 4-5, 6-7; PCIe otherwise), synthetic workloads (W3b: six short spoken questions with text + speech output; W3L: ~100-word answers; MIX: half each), `/v1/chat/completions` request-response path. "2+2+4" = thinker 2 GPUs + talker 2 + code2wav 4.
